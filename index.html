About the doctor

Meet Dr. Rohan Doshi

Dr. Rohan Doshi is a consultant dermatologist practising across clinical dermatology, dermato-venereology and aesthetic dermatology. His work centres on getting the diagnosis right first — then choosing the least invasive route that will actually hold.

He completed his MBBS at Dr. D. Y. Patil Medical College, Pune, followed by an MD in Dermatology, Venereology & Leprology at Teerthanker Mahaveer University, Delhi. Training rotations at tertiary-care centres including KEM Hospital and Manipal Hospital gave him exposure to a wide spread of dermatological disease — from routine acne through to complex immunobullous and pigmentary conditions.

My approach combines evidence-based medical treatment with appropriate procedural intervention, tailored to each patient's skin and expectations.
— Dr. Rohan Doshi

How a consultation works

Every visit starts with history and examination — dermoscopy or Wood's lamp where it adds information, and investigations only when they change the plan. You leave with a written regimen, realistic timelines, and a clear idea of what to expect before the next review.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

What happens at the first consultation?
A detailed history and clinical examination, dermoscopy or Wood's lamp assessment where relevant, and a discussion of what is driving your concern. You leave with a written treatment plan, expected timelines and the cost of each step before anything begins.
How long before I see results?
It depends entirely on the condition. Acne typically responds over 8–12 weeks, pigmentation over 3–6 months, and hair-loss protocols need at least 4–6 months before regrowth can be judged fairly. You will be given a realistic timeline at the consultation rather than an optimistic one.
Are laser and injectable treatments safe for Indian skin?
Yes, when the device settings and the patient are both chosen correctly. Higher Fitzpatrick skin types carry a greater risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, so parameters are adjusted, a test patch is used where appropriate, and photoprotection is non-negotiable afterwards.
Will I be pushed into procedures I don't need?
No. A good proportion of dermatology is managed with medication and a correct skincare routine alone. Procedures are recommended only where they add something medication cannot — and you will always be told what happens if you choose to wait.
Is a venereology consultation confidential?
Completely. Consultations take place in a private room, records are kept confidential under standard medical practice, and no detail is shared with anyone — including family members — without your explicit permission.
Do you offer online or follow-up consultations?
Follow-up reviews for established patients can often be done remotely where no examination is needed. A first consultation for an undiagnosed skin problem should be in person — examination findings change the diagnosis more often than people expect.
